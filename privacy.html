Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Freelance Ledger is a static website. There are no accounts and we do not collect, store, or sell personal data.

Hosting logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard server data (such as IP address, browser, and pages requested) for security and operations. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Calculators and newsletter

The calculators run entirely in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. The newsletter form is not active and submits no data.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you click one, the third-party site may set its own cookies to track the referral, governed by that site's privacy policy.

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-friendly service that measures visits without cookies or tracking individuals.
