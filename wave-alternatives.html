Wave (affiliate link) is our usual first recommendation for solo freelancers in the US and Canada: Starter is $0, Pro is $19 a month or $190 a year, and card payments on Starter cost 2.9% + $0.60. It isn't right for everybody. Here is when to look elsewhere, and where.

Reasons people leave Wave

  • You bill by the hour and want built-in time tracking and projects.
  • You're outside the US or Canada or invoice in several currencies.
  • You want fewer processing fees or a different payment mix.
  • You need bank-feed automation without paying for Pro, or you've outgrown basic reports.

How to switch without a mess

  1. Export your customers, invoices and transactions from Wave before you cancel.
  2. Switch at the start of a quarter or year so your books stay clean.
  3. Run both for a month if you have recurring invoices.
  4. Keep your old records. You may need them for tax purposes for years after.
  5. Check payment settings. Clients with saved cards may need to re-authorize.

Tools that help

If your problem is bookkeeping rather than invoicing, Keeper (affiliate link) scans your card and bank transactions for possible write-offs, and its tax plans include federal and state filing. Pricing is on Keeper's site; we last checked it on October 14, 2026 (14-day free trial).

FAQ

Is Wave really free?

Wave's Starter plan is $0. You pay per transaction when clients pay by card, and for optional add-ons.

What is the cheapest alternative?

Zoho Invoice has a free plan with limits (2 users, 3 projects, 500 invoices a year when we checked). Invoice Ninja also has a free tier.

Can I export from Wave?

Wave lets you export your data, but formats and what is included change, so check Wave's help pages and export before canceling.
