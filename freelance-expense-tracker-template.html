Download the 2026 tracker (.xlsx)

No signup or email. To use it in Google Sheets, upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Sheets, or use File > Import. We tested it in LibreOffice; we haven't tested every Excel version.

What's inside

  • Expenses: date, vendor, category (dropdown), amount, business-use percentage and a receipts column. The deductible amount is calculated for you.
  • Income: each payment by client and invoice number.
  • Mileage: trip log using the 2026 IRS rates (72.5¢ through June 30 and 76¢ from July 1), calculated by trip date.
  • Summary: income, expenses and mileage by month and category, and an estimated net profit.

Using it well

  1. Log expenses weekly, not at tax time. Snap a photo of each receipt and note the file name.
  2. For meals enter 50% as the business use; for a phone or internet bill enter your business share.
  3. Use the net profit on the Summary sheet as your input to the quarterly tax calculator.
  4. Back up the file. Keep it for at least three years after filing the return it supports, as the IRS generally advises.

The categories are common Schedule C expense headings. Which expenses are deductible, and how much, depends on IRS rules; see our deductions checklist. The summary is a simple profit figure and ignores depreciation, home office and other adjustments your tax preparer may apply.

FAQ

Will it work for 2027?

Change the tax year on the Summary sheet and the mileage rates on the Mileage sheet once the IRS announces 2027 rates.

Does it file my taxes?

No. It organizes your numbers so you or your preparer can.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. It is a file on your computer; we never see what you enter.
