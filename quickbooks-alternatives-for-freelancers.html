When QuickBooks is still the right call

  • Your accountant or bookkeeper requires it or works fastest in it.
  • You have employees, inventory or many bank accounts.
  • You need integrations that only QuickBooks supports.

Do you need accounting software at all?

A solo freelancer on the cash method often needs three things: invoices, a record of expenses and a profit number for taxes. A good invoicing app plus our free expense tracker spreadsheet covers that. Add accounting software when volume, an accountant or lenders require it.

Choosing

  1. Ask your accountant which tools they accept before switching.
  2. Count your monthly transactions. Under a few dozen, a spreadsheet may be fine.
  3. Take advantage of free trials and import a month of real data.

FAQ

Is QuickBooks worth it for a freelancer?

If your accountant uses it or you need payroll or inventory, yes. For simple freelancing, cheaper tools usually cover invoices and expenses.

Can I switch away from QuickBooks?

QuickBooks supports exporting reports and lists. Plan the switch at a year or quarter end and ask your accountant what they need from the old books.

What about doing my own books in a spreadsheet?

It can work for a low volume of transactions on the cash method, as long as you reconcile to your bank statements.
