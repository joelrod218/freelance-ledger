HoneyBook vs FreshBooks for Freelancers (2026)

HoneyBook and FreshBooks both let a solo freelancer send invoices and get paid online, so they land on the same shortlist. They start from different places. HoneyBook is client-relationship software built around booking work: lead forms, proposals, contracts with e-signatures, a scheduler and automations, with a 30-day trial. FreshBooks is invoicing-first accounting software: from its Plus plan you get double-entry reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access, with tax-time reports on every plan.

The short answer

  • Pick FreshBooks if you want invoices and real books in one app, you bill a manageable number of clients (Lite caps you at 5, Plus at 50), and you care about tax-time reports or an accountant login.
  • Pick HoneyBook if you book clients through inquiries and discovery calls (photographers, coaches, planners, creatives), need contracts and e-signatures on every new client, or want lead forms, a scheduler and automations in the same place you invoice.
  • Payment fees are close for US card payments. ACH is cheaper on FreshBooks (1% vs 1.5%), so it matters on big invoices paid by bank transfer. HoneyBook says it charges no international fees.

Who should pick which

  • Photographers, coaches, planners and other booked-session work: HoneyBook. Lead forms, the scheduler and automations are the reason to pay more; Essentials ($49 a month yearly) is where those fully open up.
  • Designers, developers and consultants who want books with their invoices: FreshBooks. Lite if you have 5 or fewer clients; Plus if you want accounting reports, accountant access or more clients.
  • Lots of small clients: HoneyBook, because it has no client cap; FreshBooks would push you to Plus or Premium.
  • Clients abroad: HoneyBook on fees, since it says it charges no international fees. Or keep FreshBooks and ask overseas clients to pay by wire you mark paid by hand.
  • You mainly need invoices and nothing else: you may not need either. See the best invoicing apps (some are free) or our free invoice generator.

Tax points that apply either way

  • The subscription is usually deductible. IRS Publication 334 says a business expense must be ordinary (common and accepted in your field) and necessary (helpful and appropriate). Client-management or invoicing software and payment processing fees for your business generally qualify; see our freelance tax deductions checklist.
  • Neither app files your taxes. FreshBooks gives you tax-time reports and a Profit and Loss report for Schedule C, and HoneyBook tracks income and expenses, but both leave the return to you or a preparer. If you want deductions found and a return filed for you, Keeper (affiliate link) Standard is $199 a year, and Bookkeeping Only is $20 a month, with a 14-day free trial (prices as of Nov 6, 2026).
  • Pay estimated taxes on what clients pay you. The IRS estimated taxes page says sole proprietors generally must make estimated payments if they expect to owe $1,000 or more when they file. Our tax set-aside calculator shows how much of each payment to move aside, and HoneyBook’s savings buckets or a separate account can hold it.
  • Fewer 1099s doesn’t mean less income. Publication 334 says the information-reporting threshold rises to $2,000 for payments made after 2025, so you may get fewer 1099-NEC forms. Card payments run through either app can also show up on a 1099-K. Report all of it either way (see 1099-NEC vs 1099-K).

Our take

For a solo freelancer who wants invoicing and real bookkeeping in one place, FreshBooks is the stronger pick, and the 4-month discount makes Lite cheap to start. Budget for Plus ($43 a month) if you’ll have more than 5 clients or want accounting reports and accountant access. For a freelancer whose work runs on inquiries, booked sessions, contracts and automations, HoneyBook earns its higher price—especially Essentials at $588.00 a year—and its no-international-fee policy helps if clients pay from abroad. Both offer a full 30-day trial, so run one real client through each before you commit.

FAQ

Is HoneyBook or FreshBooks cheaper for a solo freelancer?

FreshBooks is cheaper for invoicing and books. As of Nov 6, 2026, FreshBooks Lite lists at $23 a month (about $202 in year one with the 80% off for 4 months, then $276 a year). HoneyBook Starter is $29 a month billed yearly ($348 a year) or $36 month to month. The catch: FreshBooks Lite caps you at 5 clients; more than that means Plus at $43 a month. HoneyBook has no client limit on any plan.

Does FreshBooks have contracts like HoneyBook?

HoneyBook includes proposals, contracts with e-signatures and a template gallery on every plan, and will convert your existing contracts and invoices into templates for free after you subscribe. FreshBooks lists estimates on every plan and proposals, retainers and e-signatures from Plus, but its pricing page doesn't list contract templates. If signed contracts are central to how you onboard clients, HoneyBook covers that in one place.

Which has lower payment fees?

For a typical US card payment they're almost identical: FreshBooks Payments charges 2.9% + $0.30 and HoneyBook's payments page lists 2.9% + 25 cents (its pricing page also says card fees start at 2.7% + 10 cents for tap to pay). ACH bank transfer is cheaper on FreshBooks at 1% versus 1.5% on HoneyBook. HoneyBook says it charges no international or premium card brand fees; FreshBooks doesn't break those out the same way on its pricing page.

Which is better for bookkeeping and taxes?

FreshBooks. From Plus it lists double-entry accounting reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access, and every plan includes tax-time reports. HoneyBook tracks expenses, has a tax hub and savings buckets, and syncs payments to QuickBooks Online from Essentials ($49 a month yearly), but it is not a full ledger. If you want books and invoices in one app, FreshBooks Plus is the closer fit.

Is there a free trial?

Both offer a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. FreshBooks also has a 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. HoneyBook has a 60-day money-back guarantee after you subscribe.
