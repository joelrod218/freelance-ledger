Bonsai vs HoneyBook for Freelancers (2026)

By Freelance Ledger · Published November 5, 2026 · How we research

Prices, plan features and payment fees checked on the official Bonsai pricing page, Bonsai payment fees help article (updated Sept 18, 2026), HoneyBook pricing page and HoneyBook payments page on November 5, 2026. Prices and promotions change; confirm on the vendor’s site before you buy. Tax points checked against IRS Publication 334 and irs.gov. General information, not tax advice.

Bonsai and HoneyBook both promise the same thing to a solo freelancer: one place to send a proposal, get a contract signed, invoice and get paid. Neither is accounting software first, which is what makes them a fair head-to-head. The differences are price, how they charge for payments, and what each one is built around. Bonsai grew out of freelance contracts and leans toward project work: time tracking, tasks and an hourly or retainer billing flow. HoneyBook leans toward booking clients: lead forms, a scheduler, automations and a polished client experience, with a 30-day trial.

The short answer

  • Pick Bonsai if you bill by the hour or by project, want time tracking built in, and want the cheaper plan ($19 a month on annual billing for Essentials).
  • Pick HoneyBook if you book clients through inquiries and discovery calls (photographers, coaches, event and creative pros), want automations and SMS reminders, or get paid by clients abroad, since HoneyBook says it charges no international fees.
  • Payment fees are close for US card payments. ACH is cheaper on Bonsai (1% vs 1.5%), so it matters on big invoices paid by bank transfer.

Who should pick which

  • Developers, designers and consultants billing hours or retainers: Bonsai Essentials. Time tracking feeds the invoice, and it's the cheaper plan.
  • Photographers, coaches, planners and other booked-session work: HoneyBook. Lead forms, the scheduler and automations are the reason to pay more; Essentials ($49 a month yearly) is where the scheduler and automations fully open up.
  • Clients outside your country: HoneyBook, on fees, since Bonsai adds 2.5% FX. Or keep Bonsai and ask overseas clients to pay by wire you mark paid by hand.
  • You want real bookkeeping: neither is a ledger. Pair either with accounting software (see best accounting software for freelancers), or compare FreshBooks vs Bonsai if you want invoicing and books in one app.
  • You only need invoices: you probably don't need either. See the best invoicing apps (some are free) or our free invoice generator.

Tax points that apply either way

  • The subscription is usually deductible. IRS Publication 334 says a business expense must be ordinary (common and accepted in your field) and necessary (helpful and appropriate). Client-management software and payment processing fees for your business generally qualify; see our freelance tax deductions checklist.
  • Neither app files your taxes. Both track income and expenses, but the return is still on you or a preparer. If you want deductions found and a return filed for you, Keeper (affiliate link) Standard is $199 a year, and Bookkeeping Only is $20 a month, with a 14-day free trial (prices as of Nov 5, 2026).
  • Pay estimated taxes on what clients pay you. The IRS estimated taxes page says sole proprietors generally must make estimated payments if they expect to owe $1,000 or more when they file. Our tax set-aside calculator shows how much of each payment to move aside, and HoneyBook's savings buckets or a separate account can hold it.
  • Fewer 1099s doesn't mean less income. Publication 334 says the information-reporting threshold rises to $2,000 for payments made after 2025, so you may get fewer 1099-NEC forms. Card payments run through either app can also show up on a 1099-K. Report all of it either way (see 1099-NEC vs 1099-K).

Our take

For most solo freelancers who bill time or projects, Bonsai Essentials on an annual plan is the better value: contracts, invoices, time tracking and expense tracking for $228.00 a year, with cheaper ACH. HoneyBook costs more but earns it if your business runs on inquiries and booked sessions, where its lead forms, scheduler and automations save real time, and its no-international-fee policy helps if clients pay from abroad. HoneyBook's 30-day trial is long enough to run a real client through it; Bonsai's 7-day trial means setting it up the day you start.

FAQ

Is Bonsai or HoneyBook cheaper for a solo freelancer?

Bonsai is cheaper. As of Nov 5, 2026, Bonsai Essentials, its cheapest plan with invoicing and contracts, is $19 a month billed annually ($228 a year) or $25 month to month. HoneyBook Starter is $29 a month billed yearly ($348 a year) or $36 month to month. Bonsai's cheapest plan, Basic, doesn't include invoicing, so compare Essentials with Starter.

Which has lower payment fees?

For a typical US card payment they're almost identical: Bonsai Payments charges 2.9% + $0.30 and HoneyBook's payments page lists 2.9% + 25 cents. Bank transfers (ACH) are cheaper on Bonsai at 1% ($1 minimum) versus 1.5% on HoneyBook. For clients abroad it flips: Bonsai adds a 2.5% foreign-exchange fee on international payments, while HoneyBook says it charges no international fees.

Do both include contracts and e-signatures?

Yes. Bonsai Essentials includes proposals, contracts and all templates. Every HoneyBook plan includes proposals, contracts with e-signatures, invoices and a template gallery, and HoneyBook will convert your existing contracts and invoices into templates for free after you subscribe.

Which one is better for bookkeeping?

Neither is full accounting software. Bonsai Essentials tracks expenses and income, and Premium ($29 a month annually) integrates with QuickBooks and Xero. HoneyBook's plan comparison lists expense tracking, a tax hub and financial reports, and its QuickBooks Online integration starts on Essentials ($49 a month annually). Many freelancers pair either one with a separate bookkeeping or tax app.

Is there a free trial?

HoneyBook offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required and a 60-day money-back guarantee after you subscribe. Bonsai's trial is 7 days of full access, after which you choose a plan to keep using it.
