Why the January 2028 date looks odd

The fourth payment is normally due January 15. In 2028 that falls on a Saturday, and the following Monday, January 17, is Martin Luther King Jr. Day, a federal holiday. When a due date lands on a weekend or legal holiday, it moves to the next business day, which is Tuesday, January 18, 2028.

A shortcut for the January payment

Under the Form 1040-ES instructions, you don't have to make the January payment if you file your tax return for that year by January 31 and pay the entire balance with it. Many freelancers prefer to just make the payment.

FAQ

Are the 2027 dates official?

They follow the standard rule (the 15th of April, June, September and January, moved for weekends and holidays). The IRS confirms them on the 2027 Form 1040-ES, normally published later.

What happens if I pay late?

The IRS may charge an underpayment penalty that works like interest on the amount paid late. Pay as soon as you can to stop it growing.

Does the calendar file include the amounts?

No. The 2027 amounts depend on your 2027 income. Use the quarterly tax calculator to get an estimate and its own calendar with amounts for the 2026 payments.
