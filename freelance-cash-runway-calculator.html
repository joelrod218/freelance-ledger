Your cash and costs

How to read it

  • No income is your worst case: how long you can go if every client disappears.
  • Slow months show how far a thin stretch stretches your cash.
  • Many people aim for a few months of expenses in savings. How many is a personal call that depends on how steady your clients are.

Ways to lengthen your runway

  1. Cut or pause fixed costs you can live without.
  2. Invoice faster and chase late payers; see our late payment email templates.
  3. Keep a separate tax account so spending money isn't tax money (Profit First).
  4. Line up work earlier by keeping a short list of past clients to contact.

FAQ

What should my tax reserve be?

Use the tax you expect to owe but haven't paid yet. The quarterly tax calculator gives a federal estimate.

Why does average income say "indefinite"?

If your income in that scenario covers your expenses, you aren't spending down your cash, so there is no end date.
