Found vs Novo for Freelancers (2026)

Found and Novo are two of the most common online business accounts for freelancers. Both are fintech companies that partner with an FDIC-insured bank, both have a $0 checking option, and both let you send invoices and split money into sub-accounts. They differ in what they charge for and where they put their effort: Found builds bookkeeping and tax tools into the account and sells optional paid plans that add interest, while Novo keeps checking free with no plans at all and pays interest only on a separate savings account once you hold $50,000. Here is how they compare for a US freelancer, as of November 12, 2026.

The short answer

  • Pick Found if you are a sole proprietor who wants tax help built in (real-time tax estimates, a tax savings pocket, write-off finding and Schedule C generation on the free plan), you have no EIN yet, or you sometimes deposit cash ($2 per deposit).
  • Pick Novo if you want plain free checking with free standard ACH, cheaper same-day transfers on bigger amounts ($20 maximum), reimbursed ATM fees (up to $7 a month), and you keep $50,000 or more in reserve that could earn 2% APY with no plan fee.
  • Interest: Found Plus tops out at $300 a year of interest (1.50% on $20,000), less than its $315 yearly price, so buy it for the other perks, not the yield. Found Pro (2.50% on everything, $720 a year) only beats Novo savings above about $144,000.

Getting paid by clients

Both let you send unlimited invoices for free, and clients can always pay by bank transfer straight to your account details at no cost to you. Card payments run through a processor at its own rates:

  • Found: its fee schedule lists Stripe’s charges on invoice payments as 2.9% + $0.30 for cards, 0.8% per transfer plus $1.50 per bank link for direct debit, and 3.1% + $0.30 for Cash App Pay. Tap to Pay in person is 2.7% + $0.05 plus $0.10 per tap.
  • Novo: clients can pay by ACH, card (via Stripe or Square), PayPal or Venmo, and the processor’s fees apply. Novo itself charges no fee for receiving P2P payments, and Novo Boost can land Stripe payouts up to 2 business days early.

On a $2,000 invoice paid by card through Stripe’s standard rate, that is about $58.30 either way, so card fees shouldn’t decide this. If you already invoice from another app, either account simply receives the payout.

FDIC insurance

Neither Found nor Novo is a bank. Found’s banking is provided by Lead Bank (Kansas City, MO; FDIC certificate 8283), insured up to $250,000 per depositor for each ownership category. Novo’s is provided by Middlesex Federal Savings, F.A. (Somerville, MA; FDIC certificate 28368); Novo says its deposit network with Cambr can spread deposits across other insured banks for up to $5,000,000 in coverage, falling back to $250,000 if that network becomes unavailable. Two things to know: FDIC insurance covers the failure of the insured bank, not of the fintech app, and the FDIC’s deposit insurance guide treats a sole proprietorship’s account as the owner’s single account, added together with your personal single accounts at the same bank.

Who should pick which

  • New freelancer, no EIN, wants taxes handled as you go: Found. The free plan estimates your tax in real time and moves a share of income into a Taxes Pocket. Pair it with our tax set-aside calculator.
  • You pay quarterly taxes and want to do it from the bank app: Found Plus (Schedule C filers only), or pay free with IRS Direct Pay from either account.
  • You handle cash: Found ($2 per deposit). Novo doesn’t take cash.
  • You move money same-day or send wires often: Novo for same-day transfers over $2,000 (its fee stops at $20, Found’s express fee keeps rising to $25), Found Plus or Pro for frequent wires ($12/$8 vs up to $45 at Novo).
  • Large cash reserve ($50,000+): Novo savings at 2% APY with no plan fee; Found Pro only pulls ahead above about $144,000.
  • You want to budget with sub-accounts: either. Found has up to 20 Pockets; Novo has up to 20 Reserves with automatic percentage rules. See our Profit First calculator for a split.
  • You want the most account choices: see best business bank accounts for freelancers (Mercury, Relay, Lili and others).

Tax points that apply either way

  • Estimated taxes. The IRS estimated taxes page says sole proprietors generally must make estimated payments if they expect to owe $1,000 or more when they file. Found’s estimates and Novo’s Reserves both help you hold the money; see our quarterly estimated taxes guide and 2027 due dates.
  • Interest is income. Interest from Found Plus, Found Pro or Novo savings is taxable; plan fees and bank fees for a business account are generally deductible business expenses (see our tax deductions checklist).
  • Neither files your return. Found can generate a Schedule C, but you still file the return yourself or with a preparer. If you want deductions found and a return prepared and filed for one yearly price, Keeper (affiliate link) Standard is $199 a year (federal plus up to 2 state returns) and Bookkeeping Only is $20 a month, with a 14-day free trial (as of Nov 12, 2026).

Our take

For most sole-proprietor freelancers starting out, Found’s free plan is the more useful account: it opens with just your SSN and does real tax and bookkeeping work at no cost. Skip Found Plus unless you want its specific perks, since the interest can’t cover the fee. Novo is the better fit if you already have an EIN, want free checking without upsells, move money same-day in larger amounts, or keep a $50,000+ cushion that can earn 2% APY. Both are free to open, so the low-risk move is to open one, route a client payment into it, and see which app you actually like using.

FAQ

Is Found or Novo free?

Both have a $0 option. As of Nov 12, 2026, Found's basic plan has no monthly fee, and Found Plus ($35 a month or $315 a year) and Found Pro ($80 a month or $720 a year) are optional. Novo Business Checking has no monthly maintenance fee and no minimum balance requirement, and Novo has no paid plans. Both charge for some services, such as outgoing wires and same-day transfers.

Which pays more interest, Found or Novo?

It depends on your balance. Found's free plan and Novo checking pay no interest. Found Plus pays 1.50% APY on up to $20,000, which is at most $300 a year, less than the $315 yearly plan price. Found Pro pays 2.50% APY on all balances but costs $720 a year, so it only comes out ahead above about $28,800. Novo Business Savings pays 2% APY with no fee, but only on days your balance ends at $50,000 or more; below that it earns nothing. All rates are variable.

Can a sole proprietor open Found or Novo without an EIN?

Found, yes: its FAQ says sole proprietors without an EIN can open an account with their Social Security number (ITINs are not accepted). Novo's help center says most business types need an EIN, with the only exception being a single-member LLC with no employees taxed as a disregarded entity, so a sole proprietor should get one first. An EIN is free from the IRS.

Can I deposit cash with Found or Novo?

Found lets you deposit cash for a $2 fee per deposit. Novo does not accept cash deposits; its help center suggests buying a money order and depositing it with mobile check deposit (MoneyGram money orders aren't supported).

Is my money FDIC insured at Found and Novo?

Neither is a bank. Found's banking services are provided by Lead Bank, Member FDIC, insured up to $250,000 per depositor for each ownership category. Novo's are provided by Middlesex Federal Savings, F.A., Member FDIC, and Novo says a deposit network can extend coverage to up to $5,000,000 per depositor, reverting to $250,000 if that network becomes unavailable. FDIC insurance covers the failure of the insured bank, not of the fintech company.
