FreshBooks vs Bonsai for Freelancers (2026)

FreshBooks and Bonsai both let a solo freelancer send invoices, take card and bank payments and track expenses, so they end up on the same shortlist. They start from different places. FreshBooks is invoicing-first accounting software: from its Plus plan you get double-entry reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access. Bonsai is client and project management: proposals, contracts, forms, scheduling, a client portal and time tracking, with invoicing and expense tracking built in but no full accounting ledger on its plan list.

The short answer

  • Pick FreshBooks if you want your books, tax-time reports and an accountant login in the same app you invoice from, and you bill a manageable number of clients (Lite caps you at 5, Plus at 50).
  • Pick Bonsai if your week is proposals, contracts, scheduling calls and tracking project time, and an accountant (or a separate app) handles the books. Bonsai has no client limit on any plan.
  • Either way, the payment fees are the same for typical US card and ACH payments, so choose on workflow, not fees.

Who should pick which

  • Designers, developers and consultants with a few retainer clients: either works. FreshBooks Lite if you have 5 or fewer clients and want tax-time reports; Bonsai Essentials if contracts and proposals are part of every new client.
  • Lots of small clients (photographers, tutors, coaches): Bonsai, because it has no client cap; FreshBooks would push you to Plus or Premium.
  • You want an accountant to work in your books: FreshBooks Plus, or Bonsai Premium connected to QuickBooks or Xero (two subscriptions).
  • Clients abroad: check the FX fee. Bonsai lists 2.5% on international payments through its payments; for big invoices a bank wire marked paid manually (no Bonsai fee) may be cheaper.
  • You mainly need invoices and nothing else: you may not need either. See the best invoicing apps (some are free) or our free invoice generator.

Tax points that apply either way

  • The subscription is usually deductible. IRS Publication 334 says a business expense must be ordinary (common and accepted in your field) and necessary (helpful and appropriate). Invoicing software and payment processing fees for your business generally qualify; see our freelance tax deductions checklist.
  • Neither app files your taxes. FreshBooks gives you tax-time reports and a Profit and Loss report for Schedule C, and Bonsai tracks income and expenses, but both leave the return to you or a preparer. If you want deductions found and a return filed for you, Keeper (affiliate link) Standard is $199 a year (federal plus up to 2 state returns, reviewed and signed by a tax pro; prices as of Oct 28, 2026).
  • Pay estimated taxes on what clients pay you. The IRS estimated taxes page says sole proprietors generally must make estimated payments if they expect to owe $1,000 or more when they file. Our tax set-aside calculator tells you how much of each payment to move aside.
  • Fewer 1099s doesn't mean less income. Publication 334 says the information-reporting threshold rises to $2,000 for payments made after 2025, so you may get fewer 1099-NEC forms. Payments run through an app's card processing can also show up on a 1099-K. Report all of it either way (see 1099-NEC vs 1099-K).

Our take

For a solo freelancer who wants invoicing and real bookkeeping in one place, FreshBooks is the stronger pick, and the 4-month discount makes Lite cheap to start. Just budget for Plus ($43 a month) if you'll have more than 5 clients or want accounting reports and accountant access. For a freelancer whose work runs on proposals, contracts and scheduled calls, Bonsai Essentials on an annual plan ($19 a month) is the better-value all-in-one, as long as you're fine keeping the actual books elsewhere. Both offer a free trial (30 days vs 7), so run one real client through each before you commit.

FAQ

Is FreshBooks or Bonsai cheaper for a solo freelancer?

As of Oct 28, 2026, FreshBooks Lite lists at $23 a month (with 80% off the first 4 months for new subscribers, so about $202 in year one and $276 a year after). Bonsai's cheapest plan with invoicing, Essentials, is $25 a month or $19 a month billed annually ($228 a year). FreshBooks is cheaper in year one; Bonsai's annual plan is cheaper from year two, unless you need more than 5 clients on FreshBooks, which means Plus at $43 a month.

Does Bonsai do accounting like FreshBooks?

Not in the same way. Bonsai's plans list expense tracking, income tracking and bank sync for expenses, and its Premium plan integrates with QuickBooks and Xero. FreshBooks Plus and up list double-entry accounting reports, bank reconciliation and accountant access. If you or your accountant want real books, FreshBooks Plus (or Bonsai plus an accounting app) is the closer fit.

Does FreshBooks have contracts like Bonsai?

FreshBooks lists proposals and e-signatures from the Plus plan, and estimates on every plan, but its pricing page doesn't list contract templates. Bonsai Essentials includes proposals, contracts, all templates, forms and scheduling. If signed contracts are a big part of your client onboarding, Bonsai covers it in one place.

What are the payment processing fees?

Both charge 2.9% + $0.30 for most card payments and 1% for ACH bank transfers on their built-in payments (Bonsai's ACH has a $1 minimum; FreshBooks footnotes its ACH fee). Bonsai lists American Express at 3.25% + $0.30, a 2.5% foreign-exchange fee on international payments, and an extra 1% platform fee if you use Stripe or PayPal instead of Bonsai Payments where Bonsai Payments is available.

Is there a free trial?

FreshBooks offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required, and a 30-day money-back guarantee on paid plans. Bonsai's trial is 7 days of full access; after that you choose a plan to keep using it.
